First public release.

Syncs your RefDaily library into an Obsidian vault as plain Markdown:

- One note per paper, with title, authors, venue, year, DOI, abstract and the AI summary
- One note per daily digest, with the day's new papers and insights
- One note per report, covering weekly and monthly reviews and literature reviews
- Re-running a sync updates existing notes instead of duplicating them, matching on DOI first and RefDaily id second
- Optional auto-sync on a schedule while Obsidian is open

Notes are written with YAML frontmatter and no plugin-specific syntax, so Dataview, graph view and your own templates work on them, and they keep working if you ever remove the plugin.

Requires a RefDaily account; the free plan is enough.
